Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

KI-Autopilot is a personal app that creates short videos about AI and technology and publishes them to the creator's own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is operated by its single owner for their own channel.

What data the app uses

  • Account access tokens for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, granted by the owner through the official login of each platform. They are used only to publish the owner's videos and to read basic account information (username, channel name).
  • API keys for the services that create the videos (Anthropic, fal.ai, ElevenLabs or OpenAI), entered by the owner.

Where data is stored

All tokens, keys and videos are stored only on the owner's own phone. There is no server operated by this app. Data is sent directly from the phone to the respective services (Anthropic, fal.ai, ElevenLabs/OpenAI, Instagram, TikTok, Google/YouTube, GitHub for optional error reports) as needed to create and publish videos.

Sharing

No personal data is sold or shared with third parties beyond the services listed above, which are required for the app to work.

Deletion

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its token from the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes all data. Access can also be revoked at any time in the security settings of Google, TikTok or Instagram.
